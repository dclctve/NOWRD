# NOWRD CMS v23 — Complete Theme Engine

This release builds on v22 and extends Classic/Glass styling consistently across public homepage, article pages, taxonomy pills, search controls, cards, article media, and footer/about surfaces. The visitor's Classic/Glass selection remains browser-local; global admin settings remain the default for visitors who have not selected a mode.

## Changes
- Cohesive light and dark Glassmorphism surfaces on public pages.
- Softer card corners, layered shadows, subtle hover movement, and unified transitions.
- Responsive article/listing layouts and visitor switch styling.
- Honors `prefers-reduced-motion` and the configured motion preference.
- Includes a fallback for browsers without `backdrop-filter`.

## Validation
Static PHP/JavaScript syntax and ZIP integrity should be checked after packaging. Live browser, database, and shared-hosting behavior require staging verification.
